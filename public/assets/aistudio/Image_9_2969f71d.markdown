## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, therapeutic digital environment that intentionally departs from the sterile, sterile-white or cold-blue aesthetic of traditional clinical software. It balances academic rigor and engineering precision with human-centered warmth, psychological safety, and tranquility.

The aesthetic fuses **Soft Modern Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- **Atmospheric Calm:** Warm cream and soft mint surfaces reduce optical fatigue and stress responses.
- **Supportive Clarity:** Grounded typographic balance conveys stability and academic reliability while remaining empathetic and approachable.
- **Gentle Tactility:** Generous whitespace, fluid curves, and low-contrast ambient layering produce an inviting, non-intrusive sensory space.

## Colors

The palette provides cognitive rest while maintaining WCAG AAA accessibility for text and interactive targets.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#087F8C` — Deep Teal):** Active therapy exercises, key interaction points, prominent progress indicators, and primary action controls.
- **Secondary (`#15283F` — Dark Navy):** High-prominence headers, dark mode containers, structural grounding, and primary contrast elements.
- **Tertiary (`#DCEFE7` — Sage Green):** Calming affirmations, successful completion states, growth milestones, and therapeutic safe zones.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F2` — Warm Cream):** Global viewport background, softening luminance across extensive therapy sessions.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF` — Pure White):** Elevated interaction cards, modal dialogs, and modular data entry blocks.
- **Surface Low (`#EDF7F2` — Soft Mint):** Secondary panel groupings, inactive prompt states, and breathing room wrappers.
- **Domain Accents:**
  - **Muted Lavender (`#EDE8FA`):** Dedicated mindfulness routines, reflective journaling prompts, and guided audio modules.
  - **Soft Coral (`#F9DFDA`):** Gentle emotional check-in prompts, stress-state highlights, and non-punitive alert contexts.
- **Content Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Text: `#253447` (deep slate blue-gray, avoiding harsh pure black).
  - Secondary Text: `#718096` (muted slate for metadata, supporting microcopy, and auxiliary instructions).

## Typography

The type system pairs **Manrope** for expressive, geometric, and calm headlines with **Inter** for highly legible, neutral body text.

### Implementation Guidance
- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric structure with subtle circular curves evokes empathy and organic flow without sacrificing scientific authority. Use relaxed line spacing to give cognitive breathing room.
- **Body & Microcopy (Inter):** High x-height and clear counters guarantee readability across variable visual conditions and screen sizes.
- **Supportive Tone:** Microcopy must be set at `body-md` or `body-sm`, maintaining an affirmative, calm, and non-prescriptive tone throughout therapeutic interactions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt progressive rhythm** using a fluid responsive 12-column grid.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px` centered canvas, `3rem` exterior margins, and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column grid, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Single-column stacked flows for all interactive therapy tools.
- **Breathing Space Principle:** Spacing between interactive sections prioritizes `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to reduce visual noise and prevent sensory overwhelm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal Stacking and Tinted Ambient Diffusion**. Heavy drop shadows and sharp borders are avoided to prevent subconscious tension.

### Depth Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#FAF8F2` (Warm Cream).
- **Recessed Containers:** `#EDF7F2` (Soft Mint) at level 0 (inset or borderless flush surfaces) for contextual groupings and side panels.
- **Elevated Interactive Cards (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a diffused, tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(21, 40, 63, 0.05)`.
- **Floating Modals & Tooltips (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(21, 40, 63, 0.08)`, paired with a soft edge ring `1px solid rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.08)`.
- **Backdrop Overlays:** `rgba(21, 40, 63, 0.35)` with an 8px backdrop blur for therapeutic immersion when modal interaction is required.

## Shapes

The design uses a generous curved language (`rounded-xl` and `rounded-2xl`) to communicate approachability, biological naturalness, and safety.

### Geometric Application
- **Standard Controls & Badges (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` border radius for buttons, pill tags, and input fields.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` border radius for wellness cards, mood selection tiles, and prompt containers.
- **Modal Dialogs & Floating Hubs:** `2rem` border radius for holistic focal elements and therapeutic modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Teal background (`#087F8C`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `1rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover transitions softly to `rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.9)` with an ambient glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface pure white (`#FFFFFF`), Deep Teal border (`1.5px solid #087F8C`), Deep Teal text.
- **Ghost / Affirmative:** Sage Green background (`#DCEFE7`), Dark Navy text (`#15283F`), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Mindfulness & Meditation:** Muted Lavender background (`#EDE8FA`), Deep Navy text (`#15283F`), pill shape (`9999px` radius), padding `0.35rem 0.85rem`.
- **Emotional States / High Alert:** Soft Coral background (`#F9DFDA`), deep slate text, non-aggressive presence.
- **Growth & Check-ins:** Sage Green background (`#DCEFE7`), Deep Teal text.

### Interactive Form Controls
- **Text Inputs & Journaling Blocks:** Pure White background, subtle border `1.5px solid rgba(113, 128, 150, 0.25)`, `1rem` radius. On focus: border shifts to Deep Teal (`#087F8C`) with a non-jarring ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `22px` diameter, curved edges, Sage Green fill on active selection with a crisp Deep Teal check/dot indicator.

### Cards & Exercise Containers
- Structured on `#FFFFFF` base, padded with `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- Divided internally with subtle, tint-infused separators (`1px solid rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.06)`).

### Specialized Wellness Components
- **Breathing Guides:** Expanding/contracting concentric rings using translucent states of Deep Teal (`rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.1)`) and Sage Green.
- **Mood Spectrum Selectors:** Horizontal scrollable soft-pill arrays with micro-elevation and Soft Mint active feedback.
